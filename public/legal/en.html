1. What PokéArenas is

PokéArenas is a real-time Pokémon battle game played in the browser. It is a project made by a fan, out of love for Pokémon: an unofficial tribute.

It is free and non-profit. There are no ads, in-game purchases, subscriptions or paid advantages of any kind. Anyone who wants to can buy a coffee on Ko-fi to help pay for the server; it is entirely voluntary and gives nothing in return inside the game. It is not distributed as a download or in app stores: it is only played on this website.

Project owner: 1kts · contact@pokearenas.com

5. Privacy policy

Controller

1kts, owner of the PokéArenas project. Contact: contact@pokearenas.com.

What data we process

  • Your profile and progress: the trainer name you choose, your progress (Pokémon, items, badges, Pokédex), your ranked and Battle Tower results, and the date you joined.
  • Your account, if you create one: the username you choose (it can be a name or an email address; it does not have to be an email) and your password. The password is never stored as such: only a salted cryptographic hash (scrypt) that cannot be reversed.
  • Your email, if you sign in with Google or with an email code: we store your email address so you can sign in and, if you use Google, your Google account identifier (a number that says nothing about you). We do not store your name, your picture or anything else from Google, and no password is needed. When you ask for a code, we store the email and a hash of the code (not the code itself) only until you use it or it expires, 10 minutes at most.
  • Security log: when you connect, log in or fail a password, create an account, change your name or password, buy or sell in the in-game shop, claim rewards or finish a ranked battle, we record what happened, when, with which profile and from which IP address.
  • IP address in memory: the server also uses your IP for a few minutes, in memory only, to limit requests and stop attacks.
  • Bug reports: if you use “Report a bug”, what you write, your trainer name and, so we can reproduce it, the game screen you were on, the version, your browser and your screen size (not your IP).
  • Anonymous usage statistics: daily counters that don't say whose they are: how many sessions and minutes are played, from what kind of device (phone, tablet or computer), in which language, whether it's opened as an app and which game screens are visited.
  • If you write to us, your email address and whatever you tell us.

We do not ask for your real name, phone number, location or payment details.

Purposes and legal basis

  • Providing the service you request by playing or creating an account: saving your progress, ranked play, the leaderboards and your trainer card, and letting you sign in with your email or with Google (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Your email is only used to sign in: we do not send advertising or newsletters.
  • Keeping the game safe and fair: stopping attacks and abuse, detecting cheating, stolen accounts or duplicate accounts used to manipulate the leaderboards, and investigating complaints (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Fixing the bugs you report and improving the game (what gets played, where people get stuck, which Pokémon are unbalanced), with the reports and with totals that identify no one (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

There is no advertising, third-party analytics, commercial profiling or automated decision-making with legal effects. A suspension is always decided by a person and can be reviewed by writing to the contact address.

What is public

Your trainer name, your trainer card (titles, frame, badges, Pokémon in your team) and whether you are online are visible to any player; if you have an account, so are your positions and stats on the leaderboards (ranked, Tower and Pokédex). Guest profiles do not appear on the leaderboards. Your login username and your email are not public. Do not use your real name or personal data as your trainer name.

Who it is shared with

No data is sold or passed on to anyone. Only these technical providers are involved:

  • Hetzner Online GmbH: hosting of the server and its backups, in data centres in Germany (European Union).
  • Cloudflare, Inc.: manages the domain (DNS). Game traffic does not go through it. It also forwards emails sent to contact@pokearenas.com to the owner's mailbox (Gmail, by Google). Both companies provide safeguards for international transfers (standard contractual clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework).
  • Google (Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC): if you choose “Continue with Google”, Google identifies you and sends us your verified email and your account identifier. Google therefore knows you signed in to PokéArenas; its privacy policy also applies.
  • Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France, European Union): if you sign in with an email code, it sends that email, so it processes your address and the message with the code.
  • Telegram (Telegram Messenger Inc.): when you report a bug, the alert also reaches us through a Telegram bot, with what you wrote, your trainer name, the screen, the version and the browser.
  • If you follow the link to Ko-fi and donate, Ko-fi's and its payment processor's privacy policies apply; we never see your payment details.

Everything the game loads (code, 3D engine and typefaces) is served from this domain: your browser does not connect to any third party while you play. The only exception is if you press “Continue with Google”: only then is Google's sign-in button loaded (from accounts.google.com).

Storage in your browser

The game uses your browser's local storage (localStorage) for your session, your name, your language and the news and guides you have already seen. It is essential for the game to work. No cookies or tracking of any kind are used.

How long

  • Your profile, progress and account: as long as they exist, until you ask us to delete them.
  • Email sign-in codes: until they are used or expire, 10 minutes at most.
  • Guest profiles (without an account) are deleted automatically after 60 days without connecting.
  • The security log: 40 days. Moderation actions (suspensions, enforced name changes) are kept as long as needed to manage them.
  • Bug reports: 1 year. Anonymous usage statistics: 120 days.
  • Backups: the database, 14 days; the full server backup, 7 days.

If you ask us to delete your account, your profile, progress, leaderboard positions and security log are deleted. Whatever remains in backups disappears when they expire.

Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability or object to processing by writing to contact@pokearenas.com with your username or trainer name. To check the account is yours, we may ask you to do something only you can do (for example, set a specific trainer name in the game). If you believe your rights have not been respected, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) or to the data protection authority of your country.

Children

If you are under 14, you need permission from a parent or guardian to create an account.

6. Rules

  • No cheating: modifying the game, automating play, tampering with communications with the server or using several accounts to inflate the leaderboards.
  • No attacking or overloading the server, or trying to get into other people's accounts.
  • No offensive trainer names, names impersonating someone else, or names containing personal data.

Depending on the case, the name will be changed, the account removed from the leaderboards or suspended. Any sanction can be reviewed by writing to the contact address.

The game is provided as is, as a hobby project: it may change, reset seasons or close at any time, without giving rise to any right to compensation.

7. Contact

Content removal, privacy, review of sanctions or any legal matter: contact@pokearenas.com.
